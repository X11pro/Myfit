# Progress · `/dashboard/progress`

Design a trend screen for Myfit, reachable from the dashboard and the Progress navigation destination. Organize recorded body weight, calorie-related history, and strength data into readable chart views. When filtering by exercise, keep the selected exercise visible.

For strength, distinguish maximum logged weight, training volume, and estimated one-repetition maximum. Clearly identify the last as an educational estimate. Label axes, time periods, and units. Make sparse data legible: one entry is a point, not evidence of a trend. Show what to log next when a series is empty. Use text as well as color to distinguish metrics. Remote analytics are still being consolidated; avoid promising complete cloud history or automated AI coaching.
