# Welcome · `/splash`

Design the first screen of Myfit, a guest-first nutrition and strength-training app. A new user should understand what they can do before deciding whether to create an account.

- **Brand and introduction:** show Myfit and a short description connecting food, workouts, and daily energy balance. Do not use a sample calorie score or weight as if it belonged to this user.
- **Entry actions:** make “Continue as guest” discoverable alongside email sign-in. Explain briefly that guest records stay on this device and an account supports cloud features. Do not promise automatic migration of all guest records.
- **Returning state:** show “Open app” for a restored session. Route users with an incomplete profile to onboarding and eligible returning users to Home.
- **Language:** keep the EN / ESP selector easy to identify before entry. Use a dark blue-gray surface, one mint primary action, readable secondary text, and enough room for either translation.
