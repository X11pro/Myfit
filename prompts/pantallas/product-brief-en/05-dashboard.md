# Daily dashboard · `/dashboard`

Design Myfit’s Home screen around two questions: “How does today look?” and “What can I log next?” Show account and progress access in the top bar, a greeting, and four bottom destinations: Home, Workout, Nutrition, Progress.

| Component | Content and interaction |
| --- | --- |
| Daily energy balance | Lead with estimated intake minus estimated expenditure. Distinguish that balance from calories remaining **against the intake target**. Label the indicator and show kcal. |
| Nutrition | Show calories eaten versus target, a progress bar, and protein, carbohydrate, and fat in grams. |
| Quick actions | Make “Add meal” and “Start workout” visible text buttons opening their respective forms. |
| Today’s activity | Summarize the latest logged workout and available total, active, and rest time. Give a useful first-workout state. |
| Estimated daily burn | Show kcal, the estimate method, and a route to edit the energy profile. It is not a wearable reading. |
| Current weight | Show a recorded weight in kg or an empty value; tapping opens the daily weight dialog. Never invent a trend. |
| Recent logs | Summarize recent food and today’s sets and repetitions without duplicating the entire histories. |
| Weekly insights and history | Link to Progress. Keep recent daily nutrition summaries in an expandable history section when available. |

Use dark layered surfaces and mint action accents. Give the balance card more visual weight than secondary metrics. Provide both a first-day layout and a populated layout; avoid guilt-based language.
